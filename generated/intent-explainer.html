What the resident does

Every activity the simulator can perform, opened up one action at a time — what the person is doing, which object in the flat she touches, and whether any sensor is there to witness it.

The 27 actions everything is built from

An activity is a sequence drawn from this alphabet, and nothing else. A gesture has a length of its own — closing a door takes three seconds whether it happens during a five-minute breakfast or a two-hour dinner. Everything else is elastic: it is what the activity is actually made of, and it absorbs whatever time the day allots, shared out by weight. As for what the sensors catch: walking trips every detector along the route, working at an object stirs the one that watches it, and everything else leaves nothing but the faint pulses a still body gives off anyway.
